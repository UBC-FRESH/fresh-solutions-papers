# Cover letter draft — Journal of Open Research Software

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, **"FRESH Solutions: an adapter and exact-replay harness for
metaheuristic benchmarking"**, for the Issues in Research Software section of the Journal of Open
Research Software.

The paper is an experience and reproducibility report, not a solver competition. It describes a small
adapter contract, an exact-replay gate, and two reference adapters for QAP and TSP. The validation
reports both positive and negative results: a ten-seed QAP/BLS comparison, a frozen 20-instance TSP
breadth panel, a bounded broad-panel/LKH comparison, and a generic adapter-only layer that is
explicitly non-competitive. We report the BLS difference with cluster-by-instance and family-cluster
intervals, not the misleading pair-level interval.

The manuscript is 3,798 words, within the Issues in Research Software limit. We do not claim
state-of-the-art heuristic performance or third-party portability. The software and evidence are being
prepared as a public tagged release with a one-command replay; the repository and DOI fields are
placeholders until that release is approved.

Sincerely,

Gregory Paradis
