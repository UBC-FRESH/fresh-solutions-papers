\noindent
This supplement reports the per-instance results summarised in the main text. Gap values are
percentages to the known optimum, rounded to three decimal places. Counts, seeds, and budgets are
exact. The evidence files are \texttt{paper2/evidence/qap-expanded-100k-10seed.json},
\texttt{qap-bls-10seed.json}, \texttt{tsp-expanded-10k.json}, and \texttt{framework-use.json}.
Every incumbent listed here replayed exactly through the corresponding problem adapter.
