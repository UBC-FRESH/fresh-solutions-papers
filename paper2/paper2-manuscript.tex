\begin{abstract}
Applying metaheuristics to hard combinatorial problems is often slowed less by the choice of
metaheuristic than by the engineering around it: problem-specific move representations, exact
incremental state, replayable evidence, stopping rules, and benchmark bookkeeping are usually rebuilt
for each project. Fresh Solutions provides an adapter contract and a reproducible benchmark harness,
together with two reference implementations: quadratic-assignment robust tabu search and
travelling-salesman iterated Lin--Kernighan. We evaluate the references on QAPLIB and TSPLIB and report
the full distribution, including failures. Small and medium QAP instances are strong; the broad QAP
panel has a heavy tail. Four TSP instances reach the known optimum or a 0.383\% gap with iterated
Lin--Kernighan; the internal method does not scale to the largest TSPLIB tail. An optional external
LKH backend is reported separately. The contribution is the harness, exact replay contract, and
reference adapters, not a general-framework experiment or a state-of-the-art solver claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Operations-research practitioners rarely need a new metaheuristic. They need a reliable way to apply
known methods to a new problem, preserve exact feasibility and objective accounting, and produce
evidence that another person can replay. That work is repetitive and easy to get subtly wrong.
Problem-specific move representations, incremental state, stopping rules, benchmark harnesses, seed
handling, and evidence records are rebuilt for each project.

Fresh Solutions is a response to that engineering problem. It provides a problem-adapter contract,
exact resident state and reversible moves, local and population search paths, checkpointing, exact
replay, convergence records, and public-library reference implementations for QAP and TSP. The paper
asks a narrow engineering question: can a user add a problem-specific neighbourhood or reference
implementation and obtain credible, replayable evidence without rebuilding the surrounding machinery?
It does not claim that the generic engine has been tested as the source of the headline results.

\section{Related work}

The quadratic assignment problem asks for a permutation that minimises a flow--distance product.
Robust tabu search is a long-standing baseline for QAPLIB \citep{burkard1997}. Taillard's robust tabu
search remains a standard reference implementation \citep{taillard1991}; our QAP adapter follows the
same move-and-memory idea, with exact swap deltas and an optional delta-matrix path.

The travelling-salesman problem has an equally long heuristic literature. The Lin--Kernighan
variable-depth exchange \citep{lin1973} and Helsgaun's LKH implementation \citep{helsgaun2000} are the
standard strong baselines. We implement a bounded candidate-list LK-style exchange and use external
LKH only as an optional capability reference, not as part of the package. TSPLIB \citep{reinelt1991}
provides the public instances.

Reproducibility in applied optimisation depends on more than a seed. It depends on the compiled
model, candidate order, effect table, objective weights, hardware, and replay procedure. The harness
stores those elements with each record and replays the stored schedule through the exact evaluator.
The design is deliberately closer to a benchmark harness than to a new metaheuristic.

\section{Framework}

\subsection{Adapter contract}

An adapter supplies variables, options, exact and batched evaluation, feasibility, mutation and
crossover, and reversible moves. The engine sees only this interface. A domain-specific method can be
added as a separate module without changing the core.

\subsection{Exact state and replay}

Every reported incumbent is a complete assignment. Evaluation is exact, and the stored assignment must
re-evaluate to the stored objective. The benchmark scripts do not trust the optimizer's internal
counter; they replay the result through the adapter. A record with a mismatched schedule hash or
replayed objective is not evidence.

\subsection{Search layers}

The harness exposes exact local search, population search with crossover, mutation, and elitism, and a
hybrid handoff from a population elite to exact local refinement. The experiments in this paper use
the local layer plus adapter-specific neighbourhoods. The population layer remains available but is
not the source of the strongest results, and we do not claim a generic-engine framework experiment.

\subsection{Evidence protocol}

A reportable result names the pinned solver version or commit, the public instance and known or
best-known value, the seed and budget, the stopping reason, the exact-replay result, and the wall time.
Publication-facing campaigns use a patience rule; safety-cap hits are recorded as non-converged.

\section{QAP evidence}

The QAP reference implementation uses robust tabu search over facility swaps. The objective is
\begin{equation}
\min_{\pi\in\Pi_n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n} F_{ij}D_{\pi(i)\pi(j)},
\end{equation}
where $F$ is the flow matrix and $D$ the distance matrix. The adapter uses exact swap deltas; the
accelerated path uses Taillard's delta-matrix update for symmetric zero-diagonal instances and a
Numba general scan for asymmetric instances.

\section{TSP evidence}

The TSP reference implementation uses two layers: Or-opt/2-opt for construction and a bounded
candidate-list Lin--Kernighan-style exchange with deterministic double-bridge kicks. For a tour
$\pi$, the objective is
\begin{equation}
L(\pi)=\sum_{i=1}^{n} d_{\pi(i),\pi(i+1)},\qquad \pi(n+1)=\pi(1).
\end{equation}

\subsection{Broad panel and the LKH boundary}

Across 78 TSPLIB instances with $n\le1000$, 236 of 390 runs stopped on patience and 183 hit the known
optimum. The converged subset has median gap 0.000\% and mean 0.100\%. The large tail is weak: only
3 of 100 runs above $n=300$ reached patience, and 97 hit the safety cap. The internal method is
therefore a small/medium TSP solver.

External LKH, at its stated budget and configuration, reaches 71 of 100 optima on the same large tail,
mean gap 0.092\%, median 0\%, and worst 1.930\%. LKH is optional external research-use software, not
bundled and not claimed as Fresh Solutions' own heuristic. The comparison is capability-only, not
matched-time.

\section{Applied case}

TSA24/Prince George is a public-data applied case. The frozen build, target, and seed are published as
\texttt{ws4-tsa24-evenflow-v1} in
\url{https://github.com/UBC-FRESH/femic-tsa24-instance}, derived from public BC VRI, FADM, and
SiteProd data. The even-flow target is a WS4 scenario configuration, not an official TSA decision.

The 1,000-generation GPU-GA record has objective 8,588,725.370498; the 10,000-generation same-engine
baseline has 76,540.740733; the 100,000-generation five-seed campaign spans 512.574--518.621 (mean
515.682), with exact replay. The CPU-SA independent-search baseline is 335,044,976.675435 and is
time-limited, not converged. No partner-impact claim is made.

\section{Discussion and limitations}

The harness makes several engineering properties explicit: the adapter boundary, exact resident state,
replay, convergence records, and benchmark provenance. Those are useful even when the underlying
heuristic is standard. The strongest QAP and TSP results come from adapter-specific modules
(\texttt{qap\_tabu.py} and \texttt{tsp\_lk.py}), not from the generic engine. A generic-framework
experiment remains open.

The QAP median of 0\% is dominated by small instances; the mean and tail are the honest summary. The
large-QAP SciPy baseline is weak and mixed. The TSP ILK result is four instances; the 78-instance
panel uses nearest-neighbour starts and a different experiment. The internal TSP path does not scale
to the largest TSPLIB tail; external LKH is the strong path there. The applied case is public-data, not
partner-endorsed.

Patience is a plateau rule, not global optimality. LKH is external and research-use. The evidence
release and tag remain pending. No state-of-the-art or universal-dominance claim is made.

\section{Conclusion}

Fresh Solutions provides a reproducible benchmark harness and adapter boundary, with two reference
metaheuristic implementations and exact replay. It is useful today for small and medium QAP/TSP work
and for building applied cases. It is not yet a general-framework experiment or a universal
large-instance solver, and the paper says so.
