# Highlights — SoftwareX

- WS4 separates compiled model, sparse indexes, exact resident accounting, and population search.
- Every reported schedule is replayed through an exact CPU evaluator.
- One public TSA24 case: five seeds, 100,000 generations, exact replay.
- Factorized and evaluation-path results are reported as bounded microbenchmarks.
- The paper is a software/reproducibility contribution, not a state-of-the-art solver claim.
