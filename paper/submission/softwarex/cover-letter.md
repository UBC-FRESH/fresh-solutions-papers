# Cover letter draft — SoftwareX

Dear Editor,

Please consider our Original Software Publication, **"WS4: a compiled-state software architecture
with exact replay for landscape harvest scheduling"**, for SoftwareX.

WS4 is a Python system that compiles landscape harvest-scheduling models into a stable sparse form,
maintains exact resident accounting, runs a GPU population search, and replays every reported schedule
through an exact CPU evaluator. The manuscript is a 3,717-word software description, not a
state-of-the-art optimization claim. It explains the architecture, shows how to reuse the architecture and
replay boundary, and reports one public TSA24 case with a 100,000-generation five-seed campaign and
exact replay. The factorized and evaluation-path results are labelled as bounded microbenchmarks.

We do not claim an independent harvest-scheduling comparison, environmental validation, or partner
endorsement. The software and evidence are public release candidates with a replayed TSA24 example; a
DOI is pending.
