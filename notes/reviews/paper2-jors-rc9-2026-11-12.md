# Reviewer Report — Paper 2 (JORS, Issues in Research Software), rc9

**Manuscript:** FRESH Solutions: an adapter and exact-replay harness for metaheuristic benchmarking
**Author:** Gregory Paradis, UBC FRESH Lab, University of British Columbia
**Materials reviewed:** `paper2/submission/jors/paper2-jors.tex` / `.pdf` (8 pp., 3,792 words); `paper2-jors-supplement.pdf` (3 pp.); `paper2/submission/jors/README.md`; `paper2/submission/jors/cover-letter.md`; `paper2/evidence/manifest-v0.1.0-candidate.json`; `paper2/evidence/RELEASE-CANDIDATE.md`; `container/Dockerfile`; `container/README.md`; public release `p14-evidence-rc9`; FRESH Solutions release `v0.9.3-p13rc1`; and the prior review `notes/reviews/paper2-jors-rc7-2026-11-12.md`.
**Reviewer stance:** Independent antagonistic JORS reviewer. I checked the remaining submission blockers only: public source/asset/tag consistency, one-command replay, availability/DOI/container/CPU claims, the tabu `bur26` scope and supplement diversity table, BLS framing and cluster statistics, JORS length/focus, mixed-depth TSP disclosure, and redistribution/author metadata. I recomputed the headline statistics and reran the replay panels. I did not rerun the campaigns.
**Recommendation:** **Major revision — not submission-ready.**
**Prepared to review after revision:** **Yes.**

## Verdict

rc9 is a genuine improvement over rc7: the manuscript now splits the harness link (`fresh-solutions` at `v0.9.3-p13rc1`) from the evidence repository, states the container pins correctly, and the JORS `paper2-jors.pdf`/`.tex` and supplement hashes match the public `p14-evidence-rc9` assets and tagged source tree; in a prepared checkout with the `fresh-solutions` submodule at `4583ad3` and mounted QAPLIB/TSPLIB, `bash paper2/replay_all.sh` replays every panel (40 framework, 20 deep TSP, 200 QAP, 200 BLS, 100 TSP, 40 generic, 1,060 broad; 25 `esc8` skipped), and I independently reproduced the headline QAP/BLS/TSP/framework numbers. The paper is also candid about the tabu `bur26` weakness, the mixed-depth TSP panel, missing per-record CPU, the pending DOI, and the untested container, and its 3,792 words are inside the 3,000–4,000 limit (the 8-page PDF is above the rough 4–6-page guide, a production risk rather than a word-limit failure). It is nevertheless not submission-ready, because the public tag still advertises rc8 and “no tag/release created,” contains literal repository/DOI placeholders and a stale cover letter, a clean public clone does not run the documented one-command replay, 340 evidence records are stamped to `c7d1507` instead of the cited `4583ad3`, funding/ORCID metadata is missing, the BLS family-cluster/sign-test inference still overstates precision, and third-party instance attribution/license metadata is incomplete.

## Residual blockers

1. **The public rc9 package still contradicts its own tag status.** The files match, but the documentation a reader lands on does not: `README.md:3` says “P14 release candidate 8 (`p14-evidence-rc9`)”; `paper2/evidence/manifest-v0.1.0-candidate.json:200` says `"public release candidate p14-evidence-rc8; DOI pending"`; `paper2/README.md:177–178` says “No tag or release has been created”; `paper2/submission/jors/cover-letter.md:16–19` says the software/evidence are “being prepared” and the repository/DOI are “placeholders until that release is approved”; `paper2/submission/jors/README.md:35–41` still contains literal `[repository URL to be supplied at release]` and `[DOI to be assigned]` placeholders and `:43–72` still lists stale blocking issues; `paper2/evidence/RELEASE-CANDIDATE.md:13–21` names the old `paper2-manuscript`/SN-JNL assets rather than the JORS files; `paper2/README.md:76–81` still says “QAP robust tabu” and “14 instance means” (the manuscript says 15); and `container/README.md:3–19` gives Paper 1 `TSA24_ROOT`/`WS4_ROOT` instructions for a Paper 2 container. This is the public source/asset/provenance mismatch the paper’s own stop-ship list forbids (`notes/plans/p15-final-submission-2026-11-12.md`). **Action:** set every status string to the final tag, remove all placeholders, rewrite the cover letter to state the public release and pending DOI, update the release-candidate asset list to the JORS files, remove the TSA24 instructions from the Paper 2 container notes, add a release grep for `rc8`, `rc4`, `no tag/release created`, `placeholders until`, and `TSA24`, then re-cut the release.

2. **The documented one-command replay fails from a clean public clone.** The manuscript says “After mounting QAPLIB and TSPLIB, one command replays the evidence” (`MS p6`; `paper2-jors.tex:361–366`), but the snippet omits cloning with submodules, installing `container/requirements-evidence.txt`, and obtaining the pinned solver. The replay scripts insert `ROOT.parent/"fresh-solutions"` into `sys.path` (`paper2/check_evidence.py:12–14`; `paper2/replay_broad.py:13`) without a commit/version assertion. I cloned the papers repository without submodules and ran the documented command with mounted roots: it failed at `broad mismatch: tsp ali535 seed 1`; with no ambient `fresh_solutions`, it would fail with `ModuleNotFoundError`. The rc9 release body nevertheless claims “Public clone replay.” **Action:** document `git clone --recurse-submodules` (or `git submodule update --init --recursive`) plus `pip install -r container/requirements-evidence.txt`; add a guard that imports `fresh_solutions` and asserts the pinned commit (`4583ad3`) or the expected `replay_solver_commit`, failing closed; test from a clean clone and attach the log. This is a P0 reproducibility blocker.

3. **Evidence provenance is split across two solver stamps.** All 200 expanded-QAP records carry `replay_solver_commit=4583ad3`, while the 200 BLS, 100 expanded-TSP, and 40 generic records carry `c7d1507` (`paper2/evidence/qap-expanded-100k-10seed.json`; `qap-bls-10seed.json`; `tsp-expanded-10k.json`; `generic-tabu.json`). The paper promises that each record names “the pinned solver version or commit” (`MS p3`; `paper2-jors.tex:153–154`) and cites `v0.9.3-p13rc1` = `4583ad3` (`MS p6`; `paper2-jors.tex:344–345`). `c7d1507` is not the cited release tag and is not reachable from the public tag/branch I checked. The code trees are identical for the code files, so this is bookkeeping, not a scientific divergence, but a reader cannot map 340 records to the cited release. **Action:** re-stamp the BLS/TSP/generic records to `4583ad3`, or add a provenance table mapping each panel to its solver and replay commits and make `c7d1507` publicly resolvable; refresh the manifest.

4. **Author metadata is still incomplete.** The manuscript gives name, affiliation, and email (`MS p1`; `paper2-jors.tex:20–21`), and has CRediT and competing-interests statements (`MS p7`; `paper2-jors.tex:418–426`), but no Funding statement and no ORCID. The submission README confirms that ORCIDs and funding “are not yet supplied” (`paper2/submission/jors/README.md:37–39`). JORS’ suggested structure includes Funding Information, and the project’s own stop-ship list includes missing funding/author metadata. **Action:** add a Funding statement (“This research received no specific grant…” if true) and an ORCID for the corresponding author (or confirm the submission system does not require one), plus any acknowledgments; update the README and cover letter.

5. **The BLS inference still overstates precision.** The arithmetic is correct: the paired mean difference is −0.205 pp, the cluster-by-instance interval is −0.300 to −0.111, BLS is lower on 15 of 16 non-tied instance means, and the exact sign-test p-value is 5.2 × 10⁻⁴ (`MS p4–5`; `paper2-jors.tex:249–257`). But the paper also presents a family-cluster bootstrap interval (−0.234 to −0.163) built from only three families as a 95% interval, and the sign test treats 16 non-tied instance means as independent even though the eight `bur26` variants are correlated; the supplement shows four `bur26` instances with a single gap value across all ten seeds (`paper2-jors-supplement.tex:89–114`). “Descriptive and clustered” does not state that the family interval is informal or that the sign test’s independence assumption is violated. The qualitative conclusion survives, but the reported precision does not. **Action:** keep the cluster-by-instance interval as the primary summary; label or drop the family-cluster interval; replace or drop the p-value with a family-stratified test, or report 15/16 descriptively; state that the panel is a convenience sample. No new experiments are required.

6. **Third-party attribution and archival licence metadata are incomplete.** The manuscript says the four QAP and four TSP instance files in `paper2/instances` are included under their original benchmark terms (`MS p6`; `paper2-jors.tex:349–353`), but `paper2/instances/README.md` gives source/owner entries only for `tai12a`, `tai20a`, `berlin52`, and `kroA100`; the tracked `tai25a.dat`, `tai30a.dat`, `ch150.tsp`, and `kroB100.tsp` have no matching entries. Separately, `.zenodo.json:10` and `CITATION.cff:12` declare MIT for the whole deposit, while `LICENSING.md:3–8` says manuscript text/figures and derived evidence are CC BY 4.0 and code is MIT. JORS asks authors to state permission and owner details for all third-party content and to make the licence clear. **Action:** add source, owner, and terms for all eight instance files; reconcile the deposit metadata with `LICENSING.md` (split licences or use an appropriate “other” terms statement) before minting the DOI.

## Checked and not blockers

- **Asset/tag identity:** the public `p14-evidence-rc9` tag resolves to `dc2acdb`; its `paper2-jors.pdf` (`9e1006…`), supplement (`6b030c…`), JORS `.tex` (`6d69ee…`), and supplement `.tex` (`adf451…`) match the submitted files and the manifest. The PDF is 8 pages; the supplement is 3 pages; `detex -n paper2-jors.tex | wc -w` gives 3,792; the build log has no undefined citations or overfull boxes.
- **Replay arithmetic:** with the submodule at `4583ad3` and full QAPLIB/TSPLIB roots, every panel replays exactly: 40 framework + 20 deep TSP; 200 expanded QAP; 200 BLS; 100 expanded TSP; 40 generic; 1,060 broad, with 25 `esc8` records skipped and five `brazil58` cap hits reported.
- **Headline numbers:** QAP mean 0.30085%, median 0.04268%, worst 5.695%, 73/200 optima; BLS mean 0.09614%, worst 1.369%, 161/200; paired difference −0.20471 pp; TSP mean 0.003945%, worst 0.1846%, 94/100 optima, 95/100 patience; framework ratio 1.239 (95% CI 1.168–1.310). All match the manuscript.
- **Scope/disclosure:** the tabu `bur26` result is explicitly scoped as an untuned/unvalidated reference and is not generalized; the supplement has a diversity table; the TSP mixed depth is disclosed in the abstract, §3.3, and the conclusion; the missing per-record CPU and untested container are disclosed; the DOI is described as pending. These are acceptable, not blockers.
- **Availability wording:** the rc7 wrong-repository error and the false “container not pinned” sentence are fixed (`MS p6–7`; `paper2-jors.tex:343–359, 428–433`); the Dockerfile pins the base digest and `4583ad3`. The untested-container caveat is honest and, under the project’s plan, a post-acceptance item.
- **Length/focus:** 3,792 words is inside the 3,000–4,000 limit. The 8-page PDF (six pages of text plus two pages of references) exceeds the journal’s rough 4–6-page guide; this is a production risk, not a word-limit failure. The paper reads as an experience report, though a short “Lessons” paragraph would sharpen the fit; I do not treat that as a blocker.
- **Redistribution statement:** the manuscript’s statement that QAPLIB/TSPLIB are not relicensed is directionally correct; the residual problem is the incomplete package metadata in blocker 6, not the manuscript sentence itself.

## QA checklist

- **Evidence-only:** every substantive point above cites the manuscript, supplement, repository file, or release asset.
- **Anonymity/privacy:** no reviewer-identifying details; no third-party personal information beyond the materials.
- **Tone:** professional, constructive, and bounded.
- **Bias hygiene:** critiques are anchored to the submission and its artifact, not prestige or subfield preference.
- **Actionability:** each blocker names a location, the impact, and a bounded fix; no new campaign is required.
- **Scope discipline:** completed experiments were not reopened; only submission blockers and regressions were checked.
