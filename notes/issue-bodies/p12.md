# P12: Paper 2 Q1 framework-use evidence

## Goal

Determine whether Paper 2 can support a Q1 computational OR contribution by running a headline
QAP path through the generic adapter/engine contract, with matched quality, measured overhead, and
exact replay. If the experiment fails, record the negative result and use it to choose the honest
tool-paper scope.

## Scope

- Framework-managed QAP tabu on public QAPLIB instances.
- Standalone robust-tabu reference on the same instances, seeds, and budgets.
- QAP objective equality, wall-time overhead, exact replay, and failure disclosure.
- TSP replay through the same adapter contract as a secondary portability check.
- No solver release, tag, or paper submission in this phase.

## Acceptance criteria

- A committed runner reproduces every reported number.
- The framework path either matches the reference quality or the gap is reported as a negative
  result.
- Every incumbent replays exactly through the adapter/engine state.
- Paper 2 either gains a defensible framework-use section or is explicitly reframed as a tool paper.

## Out of scope

- Claiming state-of-the-art QAP/TSP performance.
- Bundling or claiming external LKH.
- Changing solver release state without maintainer approval.
