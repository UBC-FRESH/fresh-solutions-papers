# P12.4: expanded convergence panels

## Goal

Replace the four-instance convergence evidence with at least 20 QAPLIB and 20 TSPLIB
instances, using the same stopping rules as the current panels, without relabelling cap
hits as convergence.

## Scope

- QAP: 20 public QAPLIB instances, 5 seeds, 100,000-iteration patience, 1,000,000 cap.
- TSP: 20 public TSPLIB instances, 5 seeds, 10,000-kick cap, 1,000-kick patience.
- TSP starts: nearest-neighbour tours, explicitly different from the four-instance
  generic-local-search-start panel.
- Record exact replay, stop reason, actual kicks/iterations, and wall time.
- Keep the existing four-instance panels as the controlled framework-use evidence.

## Acceptance

- At least 20 instances per family with a stated denominator.
- Every run has an exact replay check.
- Cap hits are reported separately and are not called converged.
- The manuscript table is generated from the new evidence, not hand-copied.
- No tag, release, or submission action.
